ClassMetrics HR

Class data, handled carefully.

Effective September 27, 2026

What the app stores on the iPad, what syncs when you sign in, and what schools need to know.

01

The short version

ClassMetrics HR shows live heart-rate data for a whole PE class on one iPad, tracks time in activity zones, and turns a session into a report you can keep.

The class data you enter is stored on the iPad. If you sign in, it is also synced to your own account so the same classes appear on another iPad. Signing in is optional and the app works without it.

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. Movemetric never receives your payment details, and uses only Apple's confirmation of whether a subscription is active.

02

What the app handles

  • Roster information you enter: student names or identifiers, class periods and groupings, and optional details such as date of birth, resting or maximum heart rate, and notes. What goes in is your choice, and initials or numbers work as well as full names.
  • Student photos, if you add them. Photos stay on the iPad and are never uploaded.
  • Heart-rate readings streamed from Bluetooth straps during a session, and the zone and MVPA figures calculated from them.
  • Attendance, behaviour notes and fitness test results you record during class, and which strap is assigned to which student.
  • Your account details when you sign in: your email address and, if your sign-in provides it, your name. Nothing else about you.

Sign-in offers Sign in with Apple and Google. Sign in with Apple can hide your real address behind a relay, and that works fine here.

03

Where synced data is stored

When you are signed in, a snapshot of your classes, students and sessions is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), under your account and readable only by it. That is what lets a second iPad show the same classes, and what restores your data if an iPad is lost.

Student photos and the detailed second-by-second heart-rate readings are left out of the synced copy and stay on the iPad. Settings lets you choose how long those detailed readings are kept; summaries and other records remain until you delete them.

Sync is off when you are signed out. Everything stays on the iPad in that case, and nothing leaves it. The demo workspace uses fictional students and simulated readings and never syncs.

No advertising, no tracking, no sale of data. Class data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone outside the services that store it for you.

04

Student records, FERPA and consent

The school or district owns the student records. Movemetric processes them on behalf of the teacher and the institution, and does not use them for any purpose of its own.

Heart rate is health information. Before using straps with a class, follow your institution's policy on collecting it, and on how long records may be kept. If your district needs a data agreement in place first, email support@movemetric.org.

You can reduce what is held by entering initials or student numbers instead of full names. The app does not require real names to work.

05

Bluetooth, camera and Apple Music

Bluetooth is used only to find heart-rate straps and read their data. You can turn this permission off in the iPad's Settings app, and live heart rate will stop until it is turned back on.

The camera is used only if you choose to scan a printed class list. Names are read from the photo on the iPad itself, you check them before anything is imported, and the photos are not kept or uploaded.

Segment playlists play through Apple Music using your own subscription on that iPad. Movemetric does not receive your listening history, and what you play is between you and Apple.

06

Getting data out, and deleting it

Export any class or session to CSV from the Reports screen, at any time.

To delete a student, a class or a session, delete it in the app; the change is carried to your synced copy.

To delete your account, open Settings › Delete Account in the app and confirm who you are. This permanently deletes your account and its synced records, and clears class data and saved roster imports from that iPad. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that iPad. CSV files you exported are copies you control, and they are not affected.

If you can no longer sign in, or a school or parent asks for student records to be removed, email support@movemetric.org. Requests are handled within 30 days.

07

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top will change with it. Questions about privacy or student data go to support@movemetric.org.
