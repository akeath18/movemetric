01

What it does

Say what you ate. Type or dictate it the way you would tell a friend — "two eggs scrambled in butter and a slice of sourdough". Basal turns it into calories and macros, shows every assumption it made, and lets you fix any of them.

Your burn is not a fixed number. Digesting food costs energy, and it costs more for protein than for fat. Basal counts that alongside what your watch and phone actually recorded, so a high-protein day genuinely earns you more.

The targets correct themselves. Every week it compares what your weight should have done against what it did, and adjusts. After a month it knows whether you run hotter or colder than the textbook formula — most people are one or the other, by a few hundred calories a day.

Eat the way you actually eat. Balanced, lower carb, lower fat, keto, carnivore, paleo, Mediterranean or high protein. Carnivore means zero carbs, not "very low", and no fiber target you can never hit.

02

Where your data lives

Your food, weight and history stay on your phone, in Apple Health if you allow it, and in your own private iCloud so they follow you between your devices. We run no server that holds any of it.

One thing leaves your phone: the text of a meal you type, sent to be turned into macros, with nothing attached that says who typed it. Nothing read from Apple Health is ever sent anywhere.

Signing in with Apple is offered and entirely optional — nothing is locked behind it, and it is not what syncs your data. There is no analytics, no advertising identifier and no tracking of any kind.

04

What Basal is not

Basal is a tracker, not a clinician, and not a medical device. Its numbers are estimates, including the adaptive ones, and they are not a diagnosis or a treatment plan. It never sets a target below your resting metabolism and caps weight loss at 1% of bodyweight per week — guard rails, not supervision.

If you have a health condition that eating patterns affect, if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or if you have a history of disordered eating, talk to a professional who knows your situation before acting on anything the app suggests.
