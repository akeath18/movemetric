The apps

Tools for teaching, coaching, and teacher prep.

ClassMetrics HR

iPad

Live heart-rate monitoring for a whole class on one iPad. Standard Bluetooth straps stream to color-coded zone tiles, a leaderboard keeps students moving, and each lesson turns into a record of time spent in moderate to vigorous activity.

  • Live zone tiles
  • MVPA leaderboard
  • Interval timer and music
  • Attendance
  • CSV export

CoachSAGE

iPhone and iPad

Frame-accurate video analysis for PE teachers and coaches, built as a successor to Coach's Eye. Step through a clip frame by frame, draw on the movement, and compare two attempts side by side. Clips never leave the device.

  • Frame-by-frame
  • Drawing tools
  • Side by side
  • AI self-check

FieldTracker

Web

Field experience management for PE teacher education programs. Placements, hour logs, supervisor sign-off, and accreditation reporting in one place.

  • Three roles
  • Rubric assessment
  • Canvas integration

Basal

iPhone

An adaptive macro tracker. Describe a meal in plain language and Basal turns it into calories and macros, then adjusts your targets against your actual weight trend instead of a fixed formula.

Why Movemetric

Built by a PE teacher educator, for PE teachers.

01

Show what happened in the lesson

A fitness score tells you what a student can do. Time in a heart-rate zone tells you what they actually did in your class. That is the evidence that shows your teaching is working, and it is the hardest to collect by hand.

02

Tested in real gyms

Every app is used in real classes and real field placements: loud, fast, and short on prep time. If something slows a teacher down, it gets fixed.

03

Shaped by your requests

The next app, and the next feature in the apps you already use, comes from teachers. Tell us what would help in your gym and it goes on the list.
