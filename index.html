Approach

Built for teachers, by one.

01

Process, not just outcomes

A fitness score says what a student can do. Time in a heart-rate zone says what they actually did in your lesson. The second one tells you whether the teaching worked, and it is the harder one to collect, so that is where these tools start.

02

Built in real classes

These are written by a physical education teacher educator and used in real gyms and real placements: noisy, fast, and short on prep time. A tool that needs ten minutes of setup before class does not get used twice.

03

Shaped by your requests

What gets built next comes from teachers. If a new app or a change to an existing one would help in your gym, ask for it, and it goes on the list.

What would help in your gym? Ask for a new app, or a feature in one you already use.
