Streamlined assessment and tracking

Strap up the class and press start. The iPad keeps count, so you can keep teaching.

  • A whole class on one screen, colored by heart-rate zone
  • An MVPA leaderboard that shows who has reached the goal
  • An interval timer and playlists for stations and circuits
  • Attendance from the same roster
  • Fitness testing, with Presidential Physical Fitness Award scoring and printable certificates
  • A lesson summary with average heart rate, zone mix and MVPA per student

Built for teachers by teachers

Process monitoring

A fitness score says what a student can do. Time in a heart-rate zone says what they did in your lesson, which tells you whether the teaching worked.

Built in real classes

Tested in real gyms and placements: noisy, fast, and short on prep time. A tool that needs ten minutes of setup does not get used twice.

No tracking

None of the apps carry analytics libraries or advertising identifiers. Class data stays on the device or in the teacher's own account.
