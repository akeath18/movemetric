Requests

Tell us what would help in your gym.

Ask for a brand new app, or a feature in one you already use. Requests from teachers decide what gets built next.

Send your request by email

Tell us what you teach, which app it is for (or that it is a new idea), and the problem you want solved. A sentence or two is plenty.

Email a request

What happens next

  1. Your request is read by the person who builds the apps.
  2. Similar requests are grouped, so every vote for an idea counts.
  3. If you leave an email, you will hear back when there is news on your idea.

What makes a request useful

  • The problem, more than the solution
  • Grade levels and class size
  • What you use today, even if it is paper

Something broken?

Bug reports go faster by email. See Support for what to include.
