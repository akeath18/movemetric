Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Basal is built so that there is no copy of your body or your eating for anyone to misuse — including us. This page says exactly what that means.

01

The short version

We do not collect your data. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no third-party tracking, and no server of ours that holds anybody's food log, weight or health information.

One thing leaves your phone: the text of a meal you type. It is sent to our parsing service so it can be turned into calories and macros, and it carries nothing that says who typed it. Nothing read from Apple Health is ever sent with it, or sent anywhere at all.

Everything else — your meals, your weight history, your targets, and everything the app has learned about your metabolism — stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage that we have no access to.

02

Apple Health

With your permission, Basal reads weight, height, steps, workouts, energy burned and heart rate, so your calorie target reflects what you actually did rather than a textbook average.

It writes back the calories and macros of meals you log, and any weight you enter by hand, so your other health apps stay in step.

Health data is never sent off your device. It is read on the phone, used by calculations that run on the phone, and stays there. It is not sent to our parsing service, not sent to any AI provider, never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone.

Every permission is yours to change, in the Health app under Data Access & Devices. Turning one off stops Basal reading it. The app keeps working with less to go on — it falls back to the activity level you chose and to weigh-ins you enter by hand, and no screen is locked behind Health access.

03

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Basal keeps your log in step across your devices using Apple's CloudKit, in the private database of your own iCloud account.

That is not a figure of speech. The data goes to your iCloud, under your Apple Account, encrypted and governed by Apple's privacy terms. We cannot read it, cannot copy it, and could not produce it if someone asked us to. We operate no server that stores user data of any kind.

You can turn syncing off in the Settings app, under your name, then iCloud. Basal then keeps everything on the one device and works exactly the same.

04

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. Every screen and every feature works without it, and syncing does not depend on it — that is iCloud, and it happens whether you sign in or not.

If you do sign in, Apple gives the app an identifier that is unique to you and to this developer account, and cannot be used to recognise you in any other app. Your name and email are shared only if you choose to share them.

All of it stays in the keychain on your device. There is no server to send it to, we never receive it, and no email is ever sent to any address you share.

What signing in does: it puts a name on the data, warns you if a different Apple Account's history ever appears on the phone, and gives you one button that deletes everything.

05

Meal descriptions, and the only request we make

When you type or dictate what you ate, that text is sent over HTTPS to a parsing service we run, which passes it to a third-party language-model provider (currently OpenAI) to be converted into calories and macros. The result comes straight back to your phone.

The request contains:

  • the meal text you typed;
  • which model to use, and the app's version number;
  • a random identifier created the first time the app runs, used only to stop one device making thousands of requests.

That identifier is a random value with no connection to you, your device hardware, your Apple Account, or any advertising identifier. It is never used to build a profile, and "Delete all data" replaces it with a new one.

The meal text is not stored on our service. The provider processes it under terms that do not permit it to be used to train their models. Nothing in the request identifies you, so neither we nor the provider can connect a meal description to a person.

Repeated meals are answered from a cache on your own phone, so the same breakfast is usually not sent at all after the first time. Nothing is sent when you enter the numbers by hand.

06

The rest of the app

  • Widgets. The home-screen and lock-screen widgets read a small summary of your day from a shared container on the device. Nothing leaves the phone.
  • Weigh-in reminders. Local notifications, scheduled on the device. No server is involved, and you are only asked for permission at the moment you switch them on.
  • Dictation. The microphone button on your keyboard belongs to iOS, not to Basal. The app has no microphone access and never asks for any.
  • Export. Settings can write your whole log to CSV files you control, so you can take your data elsewhere whenever you like.

There is no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and nothing that asks for permission to track you.

07

Deleting your data

In the app. Settings → Delete all data removes every meal, weigh-in and setting from the phone, clears the synced copy on your other devices, and deletes the meals and weights Basal wrote to Apple Health.

If you signed in. Settings → Account → Delete account and data does all of the above and signs you out. The one thing we cannot do from inside the app is withdraw the Sign in with Apple permission itself — that lives in your Apple Account, under your name, then Sign-In & Security, then Sign in with Apple.

Deleting the app. This removes the local copy. Anything already synced stays in your iCloud until you delete it there, and anything written to Apple Health stays in the Health app until you remove it under Basal in Data Access & Devices.

Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for you to ask us to delete — but if you want that in writing, email support@movemetric.org and you will get a written answer.

08

Children

Basal is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children — and since we do not collect information from anyone, there is nothing held about a child to remove. Calorie targeting is not appropriate for a growing child without professional guidance.

09

What Basal is not

Basal is a tracker, not a clinician, and not a medical device. Every number it produces is an estimate, including the ones it learns from your own data, and none of them is a diagnosis or a treatment plan.

The app will not set a target below your resting metabolic rate, and it caps weight loss at 1% of bodyweight per week. Those are guard rails, not medical supervision. If you have a health condition that eating patterns affect, if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or if you have a history of disordered eating, talk to a professional who knows your situation before acting on anything the app suggests.

10

Changes, and how to reach a person

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything that alters what leaves your device will be said plainly in the app before it takes effect.

Questions about any of this, including the ones that start "but what if" — email support@movemetric.org. One person reads that inbox and answers it.
